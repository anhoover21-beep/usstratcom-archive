Incident File: NM-1947-RR

On July ████, 1947, debris was recovered in the vicinity of Roswell, New Mexico. Subsequent analysis determined materials were consistent with components of a classified high-altitude acoustic surveillance system known as Project Mogul.

No extraterrestrial materials were identified. No biological entities were recovered.

All documentation pertaining to this matter has been reviewed and archived in accordance with federal disclosure standards.
