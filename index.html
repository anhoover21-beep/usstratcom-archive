The Roswell Incident (1947)

In July 1947, debris was recovered from a ranch near Roswell, New Mexico. Initial press communications from the Roswell Army Air Field suggested the recovery of a “flying disc.” Subsequent clarification by military authorities identified the debris as materials associated with a weather balloon.

Project Mogul

In the 1990s, declassified investigations conducted by the United States Air Force concluded that the debris recovered near Roswell was consistent with components of Project Mogul, a classified high-altitude balloon program developed to detect long-range acoustic signatures from Soviet nuclear tests.

Project Mogul utilized trains of weather balloons, radar reflectors, and sensor equipment designed for sustained high-altitude deployment. Due to the classified nature of the project at the time, public explanations in 1947 did not fully describe the program’s purpose.

Biological Claims

Allegations regarding the recovery of extraterrestrial biological entities have been investigated through multiple official reviews. Reports issued in 1994 and 1997 determined that claims of alien bodies were unsubstantiated and likely derived from misidentified military test equipment and anthropomorphic dummies used in later high-altitude experiments.

Conclusion

The official position of the United States government remains that the Roswell debris was attributable to classified surveillance technology under Project Mogul. No evidence supporting extraterrestrial origin has been identified through official investigation.
